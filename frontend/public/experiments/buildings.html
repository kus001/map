Toronto mode fetches one fixed area near downtown Toronto from the city's building-outline dataset. It isn't live tile streaming. Reported City heights and OSM tagged building parts are used where possible; giant uncertain footprints are conservatively lowered rather than drawing invented towers. Missing heights use a 12 m placeholder. Facades use consistent floor/bay patterns, not surveyed materials. Streets, parks, and height-tagged building sections load from OpenStreetMap when available. Satellite ground uses Esri World Imagery tiles (subject to imagery service access); Toronto mode has no neutral base plate: if imagery is unavailable, the backdrop stays clear rather than covering the map.
